<a id="systems"></a>
### 🛠️ Systems I've built

#### Business Classification Engine

<div align="center">

![Business Classification Engine: company registry → identity-verified website discovery (99.4% precision) → crawl & clean → LLM taxonomy engine on self-hosted Qwen → NIC-2008 business profile (98%+), powering lead generation, peer comparison and AML/KYC due diligence](./assets/kscan-engine.svg)

</div>

Registry data (MCA, GST, NIC) says what a company is *registered* as — not what it does. This engine finds each company's official website, verifies it against identity evidence (CIN, GSTIN, PAN, address), crawls and cleans its business pages, and classifies the real activities into NIC-2008 codes with an embedding shortlist plus an LLM. Every stage is designed cheap-first: search, browser rendering and LLM calls are reserved for the cases that need them.

#### Corporate Entities Risk Platform

<div align="center">

![Corporate Entities Risk Platform: crawl open-source news to MongoDB, filter and de-noise, detect relevant businesses with NLP and an ensemble model, extract and filter entities with an LLM, profile risk with an LLM, then stitch into the entities risk database](./assets/pipeline.svg)

</div>

Continuously crawls open-source news into MongoDB, cleans and de-noises the stream, then detects relevant businesses with NLP and an ensemble classifier. LLMs extract and filter the entities, profile each one for risk, and stitch the results back into a central entities database — turning a firehose of raw news into structured, queryable risk intelligence.
